Reading the catalogue

This edition contains the favourable polytopes at h1,1 = 1–6, their FRSTs and the eigenfamilies of compatible divisor exchanges and coordinate reflections. The two polynomial characters have separate records. The field conventions follow Appendix A of Cao and Gao.

Follow a published ID

P2_1_T0_I1_Pplus is the positive polynomial character of exchange 1 on triangulation 0 of polytope 1 at h1,1 = 2. Its fixed locus is an O5 curve with Euler characteristic −18. For reflections, R followed by an integer identifies a nonzero lattice class realised by coordinate signs. The K series preserves the original Kreuzer–Skarke block indices at rank one. TX identifies a triangulation that lacked a historical number.

The four tables

FileRow and joins
polytopes.jsonlOne polytope. Its ray order fixes the zero-based Cox coordinate indices. The row includes the weights and finite Cox actions.
triangulations.jsonlOne FRST. Use polytope_id for the ray order. The row gives the SR ideal, divisor data, triple and ambient quadruple intersections.
involutions.jsonlOne action on a polytope, with its exchanged coordinates or reflection class. The eigenfamily table records the triangulations on which it occurs.
orientifolds.jsonlOne triangulation, involution and polynomial character. Join triangulation_id and involution_id to their respective tables.

Each bulk download includes all four UTF-8 JSON Lines tables for one rank, a field guide and definitions/coefficients.jsonl. That dictionary is keyed by orientifold_id. It defines generic coefficients and algebraic roots that appear in the fixed-locus equations. The browser opens it alongside the relevant family. Rational numbers such as “1/3” are exact.

Fixed sets and smoothness

The fixed set lies on a general hypersurface in the specified eigenspace and triangulation. For each locus impose all entries in zero_coordinates and equations together. The local labels in equations refer to that family's polynomials. A group of isolated points carries point_count, a positive-dimensional component carries euler, and euler_fixed describes the whole fixed set. An empty list certifies an empty fixed set. A null fixed_loci value can represent a missing hypersurface or an incomplete decomposition.

polynomial_parity is the sign of the Cox polynomial for the chosen representative. omega_parity is the sign of the holomorphic three-form. A smoothness verdict concerns a general member. In this frozen edition 3,602 nonempty families have an unresolved smoothness verdict. Of these, 3,601 divisor-exchange families had coupled derivative conditions and one reflection reached the time budget. Their published rows retain null. Consult the source batch or rank download for the exact published JSON.

Using the data in a paper

Give the family ID and the edition dated 2026-11-20 so the record can be retrieved unambiguously. Cite Xu Cao and Xin Gao, Toric Calabi–Yau orientifolds: constructions, fixed loci and O-plane charges.
